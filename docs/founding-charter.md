# Founding Charter of Christianosity

*Familia Ante Omnia — Family Above All*

**Founding date:** February 12, 2012 (as reported by the founder)
**Charter prepared:** November 19, 2026
**Founder:** Christian Quinones

---

## Article I: Name and Motto

The name of this religion is **Christianosity**. Its motto is **Familia Ante Omnia**, meaning "Family Above All."

## Article II: Origin

Christianosity was founded by Christian Quinones on February 12, 2012. As reported by the founder, he and his brother, Bradley Quinones, have practiced its beliefs for years. This charter puts those beliefs in writing for the first time.

## Article III: The Order of Devotion

1. **Family first.** Our family is our first priority. They are worth every bit of time, attention and love we have to offer.
2. **Loved ones second.** Our second priority is our loved ones, which includes the family and friends we hold close. We seek what is truly best for them.

## Article IV: Core Beliefs

### 1. Unconditional Devotion
We love our family without conditions. Our devotion does not depend on circumstance, convenience or reward, and we will never stop believing that our family is our first priority.

### 2. Sacrifice
We are willing to give up our possessions and our comfort to care for those we love. Nothing we own matters more than their wellbeing.

### 3. Hard Work
We work hard to provide for our family and loved ones. Diligence and persistence are acts of devotion.

### 4. Education and Advancement
We value learning and pursue a steady climb up the socioeconomic ladder. We do this to build a better and more secure life for those we love.

### 5. Protecting Family Time
Time with family is sacred. We are free to decline requests that would take time or opportunity away from our family and loved ones.

### 6. Informed Personal Autonomy
Each adherent makes their own decisions about their life, time and health. They do so after seeking good information and considering what is best for themselves and their family.

## Article V: Limits

Devotion to family is pursued through honest and lawful means. Christianosity does not teach that any means are justified regardless of the harm they cause to others.

This charter is an internal declaration by the founder. It is not government certification. It does not by itself confer any legal, employment or medical exemption.

## Article VI: Leadership

Christian Quinones is the creator and founding leader of Christianosity. See the [Founder Declaration](founder-declaration.md) and the [Certificate of Establishment](certificate-of-establishment.pdf).
